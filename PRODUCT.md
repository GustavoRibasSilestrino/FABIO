# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework and no build step (user's choice, offered alongside Next.js and Astro). Deploys to Vercel as a static site. Source lives in https://github.com/GustavoRibasSilestrino/FABIO.git.

## Users

Primary: people in Santa Bárbara d'Oeste (SP) and nearby towns who want to buy a home, and owners who want to sell one. They mostly arrive on a phone, from WhatsApp, Instagram or Google, and will judge whether to message the broker within seconds. (Inferred from the brief; audience details beyond "clients of a local broker" are unconfirmed.)

## Product Purpose

Personal website of Fábio, a real-estate broker (corretor de imóveis) in Santa Bárbara d'Oeste. The first screen exists to sell: turn a visitor into a WhatsApp conversation or a lead. Everything after the first screen works as a portfolio of imóveis and of Fábio's work. Success is a message to Fábio.

## Positioning

One local broker you talk to directly, instead of a faceless agency catalog. (Inferred; confirm with the user and Fábio.)

## Operating Context

Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR), prices in R$, areas in m². Property facts follow the local listing convention: tipo, bairro, cidade-SP, quartos, banheiros, vagas, área. Primary contact channel is WhatsApp. Brokers in Brazil show a CRECI registration number as a trust marker.

Reference sites the user supplied, as functional inspiration only: https://cipop.com.br/ (hero with financing CTA and property search, listing grid, services cards, broker cards with CRECI, floating WhatsApp, contact form) and https://www.mensoimoveis.com.br/ (utility bar with CRECI/phone, search panel comprar/alugar by bairro/tipo/quartos, highlight carousels, category tiles).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Service the user confirmed: compra e venda (buying and selling). Rental, financing and valuation were not selected and are not presented as services.
- No backend. Contact happens through WhatsApp deep links and a form that composes a WhatsApp message.
- Listings on the page are illustrative placeholders; real ones come from Fábio later.
- Undecided / to be supplied: Fábio's full name, WhatsApp number, CRECI number, Instagram, address, real listings and photos, testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

None yet. The user asked for "modern and beautiful", aimed at Fábio's clients. No logo, colors or fonts exist.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. No photos, listings, testimonials, CRECI number, phone number or sales figures were supplied. Do not fabricate testimonials, sales counts, years of experience or awards. Placeholder photography comes from Unsplash and stands in for Fábio's own photos.

## Product Principles

- The first screen sells; every later section earns trust for that first click.
- WhatsApp is the product's checkout: reachable from every section, one tap on a phone.
- Show houses, not decoration: imagery carries the page.
- Never present placeholder facts as real. Flag what must be replaced.
- Fast and light on mid-range phones over mobile data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, keyboard-operable controls, reduced-motion respected, 44px touch targets. Audience skews broad in age, so text stays large and plain.
