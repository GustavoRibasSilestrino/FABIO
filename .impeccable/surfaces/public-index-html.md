# Surface brief: public/index.html (Fábio, corretor de imóveis, Santa Bárbara d'Oeste)

Mode: Persuade for the first screen, then Experience (portfolio of imóveis) for the rest. One long page, pt-BR, mobile-first.

Audience and job: home buyers and home sellers in Santa Bárbara d'Oeste (SP), on phones. Job: decide in seconds to message Fábio on WhatsApp, or filter imóveis. Proof on hand: none real. Listings, prices, bairros, photos and the CRECI/WhatsApp are placeholders and must read as such in the handoff list. No invented testimonials, sales counts or years of experience.

Sections after the hero: imóveis em destaque (portfolio grid fed by the hero search), vender (seller CTA band), ambientes (gallery), como funciona (compra e venda), sobre o Fábio, contato (form that composes a WhatsApp message), footer with disclaimer. Floating WhatsApp button throughout.

Memorable moment: three flat arches rise from the ground line at load, then stay still; photo corners morph on hover.

Unresolved: real name, CRECI, WhatsApp, photos, copy to confirm with Fábio.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one modernist house drawn in flat colour: layered arches standing on a concrete ground line, the way Brazilian modernism drew a home worth coming back to. It refuses the category's navy-and-gold hero with a stock photo and a search bar glued on.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched jardim green (#0D5C41) hero, concreto grey-green ground (#E4E9E1), saffron (#F5B301) reserved for primary actions, the logo mark and one small sun, cobalt (#2D4BD6) as the second colour field, ink #10241C. Flat colour only: depth comes from overlapping arches, never shadow, gradient or glass. Bricolage Grotesque for display, Hanken Grotesk for text. Photos are cut into arches and single quarter-circle corners. Every listing carries the same label grid (código, tipo, bairro) set between 1px ink rules, like the end label of a box.

STORY: The visitor sees a house they want, a local person they can trust, and one saffron button to talk to Fábio. They believe he is direct and close by, and they tap WhatsApp or filter the imóveis.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Emerald field, 100svh. Left, spanning six of twelve columns: H1 "Encontre a casa certa em Santa Bárbara d'Oeste.", a short subhead, the saffron WhatsApp button beside a text link to sell, and beneath them the search panel as the screen's highlight (title "Qual imóvel você procura?", tipo, bairro, quartos, a full-width Buscar button). Right: three layered flat arches (concreto, cobalt, then the hero photo in the tallest, front arch) and a saffron sun, standing on a concrete ground line at the hero's foot. On phones (<=639px) the panel is full-bleed right after the H1, with a saffron Buscar button; the WhatsApp CTA becomes a white ghost button under it and the arches follow below. The whole page scales with a fluid root font-size.

FORM: Jardim Moderno (Brazilian tropical modernism: Burle Marx colour fields, Niemeyer curves), candidate 6 of 7 on my ordered list, assigned by the roll and kept by the user. seed:bdd38d56d402b057. Raises: layered flat colour for depth (from Papercut Łowicz), one label grid ruling every listing (from Sneaker Box Stacks), saturated fields owning whole regions (from Du Bois Data Portraits).
