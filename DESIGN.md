# Design System: Fábio, Corretor de Imóveis

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Modernist House in Flat Colour"**

The site is one house drawn the way Brazilian modernism drew it: whole regions owned by a single saturated colour, arches standing on a concrete ground line, and a single quarter-circle swung out of an otherwise square photograph. Burle Marx colour fields, Niemeyer curves. Depth is stacking, never lighting: one flat shape overlaps another and that is the whole illusion.

Density is generous and mobile-first. Sections alternate whole colour fields (jardim green, cobalt, concrete, deep green) rather than sharing a neutral page with cards on it. The voice is direct and local, and the interface is built so that one saffron button is always the loudest thing within reach: WhatsApp on most screens, the search submit on a phone's first screen.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Every section owns one flat colour field; no gradient, glass or blur anywhere in the build.
- Arches (fully rounded top, square base) and a single large quarter-circle corner are the only large-scale shapes.
- Every listing carries the same three-cell label grid (Código, Tipo, Bairro) between ink rules.
- Saffron is spent on the screen's primary action, the logo mark and one sun; nothing else.
- The hero's highlight is the search panel, not the arches.
- The whole page is sized in rem on a fluid root, so it scales up on large screens.
- Photos are placeholder stock, marked "Ilustrativo" on each card.

## Colors

A drenched tropical palette: two greens, one cobalt, one saffron, over a grey-green concrete ground and an ink that is green-black, never pure black. Pure white appears only as text on dark fields and as the fill of form controls.

### Primary
- **Jardim Green** (jardim): the hero, top bar, "Como funciona" band and hover state of ink buttons. The brand's dominant field.
- **Saffron** (acafrao): primary action buttons, the floating WhatsApp button, the sun in the hero, selection highlight, link underlines on dark fields, step 4 card, and the phone search submit. Hover lifts to Saffron Light (acafrao-hover).

### Secondary
- **Cobalt** (cobalto): the second colour field. The "Vender" band, the middle hero arch, step 1 card, focus ring and caret on light surfaces.

### Neutral
- **Concreto** (concreto): the page ground, the ground line under the hero arches, the imóveis and Sobre sections, step 2.
- **Concreto Claro** (concreto-claro): raised light surfaces: search panel, contact form, empty state, Ambientes section, tile captions.
- **Jardim Fundo** (jardim-fundo): deepest green. Photo placeholder fill, contato and footer fields, mobile nav drawer, step 3.
- **Ink** (tinta): all text on light fields, the ink button and ghost outline, label-grid rules.
- **Ink Soft** (tinta-suave): secondary text and field labels on light fields.
- **Menta** (menta): secondary text on green fields. **Azul Claro** (azul-claro): secondary text on cobalt.
- **Error Red** (erro): form validation only.

### Tints and white
- **White** (branco, #ffffff): text and icons on green and cobalt fields, the fill of selects and text inputs, and the ghost button text on the phone hero.
- **White tints** on dark fields only: 12% (branco-12) ghost-button hover fill; 14% (branco-14) rule between mobile nav rows; 16% (branco-16) footer and drawer hairlines; 35% (branco-35) menu button outline; 40% (branco-40) contact link underline; 60% (branco-60) ghost button border on the phone hero.
- **Ink tints** on light fields: 8% (tinta-8) chip hover; 82% (tinta-82) the "Ilustrativo" seal fill; Ink Placeholder (tinta-placeholder) placeholder text in form inputs.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Field Rule.** A region is one solid colour. Depth comes from overlapping shapes and adjacent fields, never from shadow, gradient, glass or blur.
**The Saffron Scarcity Rule.** Saffron marks the screen's primary action, the mark and the sun. It is never a background for a whole section and never decoration for its own sake.
**The Phone Search Exception.** At 639px and below the search panel is the primary action of the first screen, so its submit button is saffron and the WhatsApp CTA steps down to a white-outlined ghost button below it. Only one saffron button competes in that viewport; from 640px up the submit is ink and WhatsApp keeps saffron.
**The Paired Text Rule.** Light fields take Ink and Ink Soft; green fields take white and Menta; cobalt takes white and Azul Claro. Do not cross the pairs.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bricolage Grotesque (with Arial Narrow fallback), self-hosted variable woff2
**Body Font:** Hanken Grotesk (with system-ui fallback), self-hosted variable woff2

**Character:** Bricolage is tight, heavy and slightly quirky in headlines and prices; Hanken stays calm and legible for everything else. Hierarchy comes from weight and size contrast, not from case or tracking tricks.

### Hierarchy
All sizes are rem on the fluid root (see Layout). Values below are the clamp endpoints or fixed steps the stylesheet uses.
- **Display** (800, clamp(2.6rem, min(1.1rem + 5vw, 7.8vh), 5.4rem), 0.98): the hero H1, max 13.5em wide. At 899px and below clamp(2.45rem, 1.2rem + 6vw, 3.6rem); at 639px and below clamp(2.25rem, 1rem + 6.6vw, 3.1rem).
- **Headline** (800, clamp(2.1rem, 1.2rem + 3.2vw, 3.75rem), 1.02): section H2s; Sobre clamps 2rem to 3.5rem.
- **Search title** (800, clamp(1.35rem, min(1.15rem + 0.9vw, 3.6vh), 1.9rem), 1.1): "Qual imóvel você procura?" in the panel; on phones clamp(1.75rem, 7.6vw, 2.25rem).
- **Step number** (800, 3.5rem, 0.9): "Como funciona" numerals; clamp(4.5rem, 7vw, 6.5rem) at 1024px.
- **Price** (800, clamp(1.7rem, 1.4rem + 0.8vw, 2.125rem), 1, tabular numerals): the one number on a listing card.
- **Step title** (700, 1.5rem, 1.75rem at 1024px): step headings.
- **Title** (700, 1.3rem to 1.5rem, 1.15): listing names; empty-state heading (1.5rem).
- **Lede** (400, 1.125rem, 1.6): section subheads, Sobre and Contato paragraphs, footer-adjacent copy. **Lede large** (400, clamp(1.0625rem, 0.95rem + 0.5vw, 1.3125rem), 1.5): hero and Vender subheads; also the 1.25rem mobile nav links.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.6): paragraphs, contact list, selects in the search panel, capped at 28 to 38em.
- **Button** (700, 1rem; 1.0625rem large; 1.125rem search submit; 1.1875rem phone submit; 0.9375rem small): sentence case.
- **UI text** (0.9375rem, 600 to 700): chips, option pills, specs, label-grid values, search field labels, hero proof strip.
- **Note** (0.875rem, 600 to 700): tile captions, portrait caption, error messages, form note, demo note, footer base line.
- **Field label** (700, 0.8125rem): field labels and legends on light fields (1rem on phones).
- **Label** (700, 0.75rem, sentence case, no tracking): label-grid headings (dt), seals, wordmark subtitle (0.04em tracking on the small line under the logo).

### Named Rules
**The Sentence Case Rule.** Labels, chips and buttons are sentence case at normal tracking. Small caps-style uppercase labels are not part of this system.
**The Tight Display Rule.** Bricolage headings run -0.02em to -0.035em tracking and balanced wrapping; body text never does.

## Layout

All dimensions are rem. `html` carries a fluid root, `font-size: clamp(100%, 0.625vw + 0.375rem, 187.5%)`, so 1rem is 16px up to about 1000px wide, 18px at 1920 and 22px at 2560, and the entire page (type, spacing, shapes, content width) scales together on large screens. It is anchored on a percentage so a user's browser font setting still applies.

**The Fluid Root Rule.** Size everything in rem (anything over 3px); never pin a pixel size to a component. Hairlines and focus-ring offsets of 3px or less stay in px.

A single long column of full-bleed colour fields, each holding a content wrap of `min(100% - 2 * gutter, 80rem)` with a fluid gutter of 1.25rem to 4rem. The hero is a 6.6fr / 5.4fr grid (copy left, arches right) filling the viewport under a 4.5rem sticky top bar. The search panel is the hero's highlight: a three-column grid with title, three selects and a full-width submit, whose field height, button height and padding are vh-fluid (field clamp(3rem, 6.4vh, 3.625rem), submit clamp(3.25rem, 6.6vh, 3.75rem)). On short desktop screens (min-width 900px and max-height 820px) the proof strip under the panel is hidden so the panel fits. Section padding runs clamp(4.5rem, 9vw, 8rem) top.

At 899px and below the hero stacks in one column in this order: H1, subhead, search panel, CTA row, arch group (shortened), proof strip. From 640 to 899px the panel keeps three columns. At 639px and below the panel goes full-bleed (negative gutter margins, no side radius, one 3.5rem top-right curve), is ordered immediately after the H1, uses a two-column grid with the middle select spanning both, larger fields (clamp(3.5rem, 16vw, 4.25rem)) and a saffron submit (clamp(3.75rem, 17vw, 4.5rem)); the WhatsApp CTA becomes a full-width white-outlined ghost button below it and the subhead moves under the CTA.

Listings are 1 column on phones, 2 from 640px, 3 from 1024px with a 4.5rem stagger on the middle column. The Ambientes mosaic is a 12-column bento at 1024px, 2-column at 640px. "Como funciona" is four cards that become a staircase of arches rising by 4rem per step at 1024px. Breakpoints: 640, 900, 1024 (px, media-query only), plus max-height 820 for the hero proof strip. Spacing is a loose rhythm in rem (0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5) with larger gaps between sections than within. Tap targets are at least 2.75rem.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. There are no box-shadows, gradients, backdrop filters or glass in the build. Depth is conveyed by overlap (the hero's three arches, the stepped arches in Como funciona), by alternating colour fields, and by a 1rem concrete ground line under the hero (0.625rem under the stacked arches on mobile) on which the arches stand. The only translucency is the dark "Ilustrativo" seal (ink at 82%), hairline rules on dark fields (white at 14 to 16%) and the ghost-button border and hover on the phone hero (white at 60% and 12%).

### Named Rules
**The Layered Colour Rule.** To raise something, put a different flat colour behind or in front of it. Never add a shadow.

## Shapes

Two gestures, used with discipline. The **arch**: a full-pill top radius (62.4375rem) with a square base, for hero arches, the Ambientes tall tile and the desktop step cards. The **quarter-circle corner**: one corner at 2.25rem on small panels (form, empty state, step cards on mobile), 3rem on the search panel (3.5rem when it bleeds on phones), or up to 8.25rem on photos (clamp(4rem, 9vw, 8.25rem)), every other corner a near-square 0.25 to 0.375rem. Each photo gets exactly one large corner, alternating top-left and top-right. On hover (pointer devices) a listing's large corner grows by 1.6x while the image scales to 1.05. Controls are pills (62.4375rem, i.e. fully round) or 0.75rem-radius inputs with a 2px ink border. Label grids use 1.5px ink rules, top and bottom plus vertical dividers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** full pills, 2px border, min-height 3.25rem (2.75rem small, 3.75rem large), bold Hanken.
- **Primary:** saffron fill, ink text. Hover lightens to Saffron Light. Used for every WhatsApp action, except on phones' first screen (see ghost on dark).
- **Ink:** ink fill, light text, hover to Jardim Green. For the desktop and tablet Buscar and for form submission. On phones the search submit swaps to saffron fill with ink text (Phone Search Exception).
- **Ghost:** 2px ink outline, fills ink on hover. Per-listing "Tenho interesse".
- **Ghost on dark:** white text, 2px white-60 border, white-12 fill on hover. Only the hero WhatsApp CTA at 639px and below.
- **Press / focus:** scale(0.97) on active; 3px focus ring offset 3px, cobalt on light fields and saffron on dark fields.

### Chips
- **Style:** pill, 2px ink border, transparent; filter chips for property type and the option chips in the contact form.
- **State:** selected is ink fill with Concreto Claro text; hover adds an 8% ink tint.

### Cards / Containers
- **Listing:** no container box. A photo with one large corner, the label grid, title, specs in two columns with icons, price and a ghost button. Seals sit on the photo: saffron "Destaque" top-right, dark "Ilustrativo" bottom-left.
- **Panels** (form, empty state): Concreto Claro fill, one 2.25rem corner, no border, no shadow.
- **Step cards:** each a different flat field (cobalto, concreto, jardim-fundo, acafrao).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white fill, 2px ink border, 0.75rem radius, 3rem to 3.625rem tall (3.5rem to 4.25rem on phone search), label above in Ink Soft bold 0.8125rem (0.9375rem in the search panel, 1rem on phones).
- **Focus:** the global 3px cobalt outline. **Error:** border turns Error Red with a bold red message below.

### Search Panel (signature, hero)
Concreto Claro panel, one 3rem top-right corner, other corners 0.375rem. Title "Qual imóvel você procura?" (Search title), three labelled selects (tipo, bairro, quartos), full-width submit with a search icon. Spacing and control heights are vh-fluid so it fits the first screen; it is the first thing after the H1 on phones and bleeds to the screen edges there.

### Navigation
Sticky 4.5rem Jardim Green bar: arch-mark logo with Bricolage wordmark, semibold links with a saffron underline that scales in from the left on hover, a small saffron WhatsApp button. Under 900px links collapse to a circular menu button (white-35 outline) and a Jardim Fundo drawer with ruled rows. The floating saffron WhatsApp button exists only below 900px and hides while the search panel or any WhatsApp CTA is on screen (IntersectionObserver in main.js); it is an icon-only round button at 639px and below.

### Label Grid (signature)
A definition list in three cells (Código, Tipo, Bairro) between ink rules, with small bold Ink Soft headings over bold values. Identical on every listing; this is the system's recurring signature.

### Motion
One easing: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). Hero arches rise from the ground in 1.3s stagger, the sun scales in last. Content reveals on scroll with a 1.5rem rise and fade (0.9s), steps entering in sequence. Reduced-motion collapses all durations.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** give each section a whole flat colour field and keep text on its paired colour set.
- **Do** cut photos with exactly one large quarter-circle corner (or a full arch) and 0.25rem on the rest.
- **Do** keep a single saffron button as the dominant action on any screen: WhatsApp generally, the search submit on the phone first screen.
- **Do** rule every listing with the same Código / Tipo / Bairro label grid.
- **Do** size in rem on the fluid root, keep tap targets at 2.75rem or more and set focus rings by field colour.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add shadows, gradients, glass or blur; layer flat colour instead.
- **Don't** use saffron as a section background or for ornament.
- **Don't** round every corner of a photo; one big curve per piece.
- **Don't** fall back to the real-estate default of navy and gold with a stock hero photo and a bolted-on search bar.
- **Don't** set uppercase tracked kickers or eyebrows above headings; the build has none.
- **Don't** put two saffron buttons in the same phone viewport.
